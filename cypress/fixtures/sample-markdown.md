# Ocean Ecosystems Vocabulary

## Core Terms

**ecosystem** - A biological community of interacting organisms and their physical environment.

**photosynthesis** - The process by which green plants and some other organisms use sunlight to synthesize nutrients from carbon dioxide and water.

**marine** - Of, found in, or produced by the sea.

**biodiversity** - The variety of plant and animal life in a particular habitat or in the world as a whole.

**coral reef** - A ridge of rock in the sea formed by the growth and deposit of coral.
