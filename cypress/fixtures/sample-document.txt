## What is Photosynthesis?

Photosynthesis is the process by which plants make food using sunlight.

### Key Vocabulary:

- **Photosynthesis**: The process plants use to convert light energy into chemical energy
- **Chlorophyll**: The green pigment in plants that captures light energy
- **Glucose**: A simple sugar produced during photosynthesis
- **Carbon dioxide (CO2)**: A gas absorbed by plants from the atmosphere
- **Oxygen (O2)**: A gas released by plants as a byproduct

### Sample Questions:

1. What is the primary function of chlorophyll in plants?
2. What are the main inputs and outputs of photosynthesis?
3. Why is photosynthesis important for life on Earth?
