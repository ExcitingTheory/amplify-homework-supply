## Student and Parent Rights (FERPA)

Under FERPA, students and parents (for students under 18) have rights through the educational institution:

### Right to Access
- Review education records through the educational institution
- Request correction of inaccurate records

### Right to Deletion
- Request deletion of student data (subject to institutional record-keeping requirements)

### Right to Restrict Disclosure
- Control sharing of directory information (handled by the institution)

**How to Exercise Rights**: Contact your educational institution, not us directly. The institution controls student education records and will coordinate with us.

## Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our services may be used by children under 13. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act):

- We do not collect more information than necessary for educational purposes
- We do not require children to disclose personal information to participate
- Parental consent is obtained by the educational institution (school official exception)
- Parents can review and request deletion of their child's information through the institution
