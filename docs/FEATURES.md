# What This App Does

A guide to everything this learning platform offers — written for anyone, not just developers.

---

## Building Lessons

### The Lesson Editor

Instructors build lessons by stacking content blocks — text, images, videos, quizzes, matching exercises, vocabulary highlights, audio playlists, and embedded documents. It works like a modern word processor where each block can be rearranged by dragging. Students later complete these lessons as interactive workbooks that track their answers and scores.

Instructors can build rich, interactive learning experiences without any coding knowledge.

### AI Writing Assistant

As instructors type, the editor suggests how to finish their sentences — like phone autocomplete but smarter. It understands the course context (what the lesson is about, what came before) so the suggestions are relevant and save time drafting content.

Writing lessons from scratch is time-consuming — intelligent suggestions speed up the creative process considerably.

### Smart Layout Suggestions

The editor learns from how an instructor typically structures lessons and suggests what block to add next. For example, after a vocabulary section it might suggest a quiz block because that's what the instructor usually does. These suggestions appear instantly based on patterns from their own past lessons.

Fewer decisions means less fatigue, and lessons naturally develop a more consistent structure over time.

### Co-Authoring in Real Time

Multiple instructors can edit the same lesson simultaneously and see each other's cursors and selections in real time. Changes sync instantly, and everything is saved locally in case of disconnection, so no work is ever lost.

Teams can collaborate without emailing files back and forth or worrying about overwriting each other's work.

### Custom AI-Graded Exercises

Instructors design open-ended exercises (written responses, audio recordings, drawings, or image uploads) and the AI grades them using rules the instructor defines. Students get immediate, personalized feedback without the instructor needing to review every single submission manually.

It opens up creative assignments that go beyond multiple choice while still giving every student instant feedback — even in large classes.

### Vocabulary & Question Management

Instructors manage large lists of vocabulary words and practice questions with fast search, inline editing, and bulk actions. Words include pronunciation guides, audio clips, and images. Questions support multiple formats. Everything auto-saves as you edit.

Managing hundreds of vocabulary items and questions only works if it's fast and friction-free — otherwise it just doesn't happen.

### Audio Recording Studio

A guided recording tool where instructors create conversational content by following scripts (like a screenplay) and recording themselves speaking. They can see a timeline of all their takes, compare versions, and apply audio filters. The AI can even help generate scripts from lesson content.

Hearing an instructor's own voice makes lessons feel more personal and engaging — and that effect is proven to improve retention compared to reading text alone.

## The Student Experience

### Interactive Workbooks

Students work through lessons as interactive workbooks — answering questions, completing exercises, and getting scored in real time. Progress saves automatically so students can leave and pick up exactly where they stopped. If a tutor is online, students can see their cursor offering guidance.

Students get immediate feedback and never lose their work, which makes studying a lot less frustrating.

### Commenting on Specific Sections

Instructors and students can leave comments on specific parts of a workbook by clicking icons in the margin. This creates focused conversations about particular content — much better than generic "I have a question about the homework" messages.

Targeted feedback on exactly the right paragraph or exercise leads to faster understanding than general notes ever could.

### Working Without Internet

Students can save lessons for offline use and complete their work without an internet connection. When they reconnect, their answers automatically sync back to the server. This is important for students with unreliable internet or who study during commutes.

Learning shouldn't stop just because you're on an airplane or somewhere with poor connectivity.

### Student Profile Page

Each student has a profile showing their earned badges, daily study streaks, progress charts, and a roadmap of what they'll unlock next. They can personalize their avatar and show off accomplishments to classmates.

Visible progress and a sense of personal identity increase motivation and give students real ownership over their learning journey.

### AI-Powered Practice

Students can practice any material on demand — the AI generates fresh exercises from vocabulary, lesson content, and uploaded documents. It tracks what's been practiced and gradually reduces rewards for repeated material to encourage covering new ground.

Spaced, varied practice is proven to improve retention — and AI generation means the exercises never run out, with no extra work for instructors.

### Student Dashboard

When students log in, they see their assignments organized by class with clear "what to do next" recommendations. Upcoming due dates are prominent, completed work shows achievement badges, and locked content shows what they need to accomplish to access it.

Students immediately know what needs attention without digging through menus or having to ask.

### Peer Review

Students can review each other's work in dedicated review rooms. They join with a code, give feedback, and can ask the AI for help crafting their reviews. The AI also summarizes feedback, and a safety layer catches inappropriate content.

Giving and receiving peer feedback sharpens critical thinking — and takes some of the grading load off instructors.

## The AI Assistants

### Intelligent Tutoring Bots

Two AI assistants with different roles: one helps students by answering questions, explaining concepts, and quizzing them; the other helps instructors with analytics, content generation, and course planning. Both remember past conversations, can search through course materials, and respond in real-time as streaming text.

24/7 personalized support means students are never stuck waiting for office hours, and instructors get an assistant that actually knows their entire course.

### Group Chat with AI

Students and instructors chat in real-time rooms organized by class or study group. Conversations support threads, mentions, and topics. Anyone can summon the AI tutor by mentioning it, and it responds to the whole group — making it like having a teaching assistant always present.

An always-available AI in the room changes the dynamic — it makes group study sessions more productive and engaging at any hour.

### Always-Available Chat Button

A floating chat button appears on every page. Clicking it slides open the AI assistant without leaving what you're working on. The conversation persists as you navigate — so you can ask for help in a lesson, switch pages, and come back without losing context.

Help should be one click away, no matter where you are in the app.

### Personalizing the AI Avatar

Students unlock the ability to customize how the AI assistant looks — changing its colors, expression, and accessories. New customization options unlock as they earn achievement badges, giving them another reason to engage.

Personalization creates emotional connection, and having new things to unlock keeps students coming back.

### Instructor AI Controls

Instructors can tune how the AI behaves for their class — adjusting how creative or conservative its responses are, choosing which AI model powers it, and configuring its personality. These settings apply per class so different courses can have different AI behavior.

Instructors know their students best — they should be the ones setting the tone for how the AI teaches.

### Works Without Internet (AI Too)

The entire app works offline as an installable application. Beyond just loading pages, it can run AI chat and grading using small models that download to the device. Any actions taken offline (submitting work, chatting) queue up and sync automatically when reconnected.

True offline support — AI included — means the platform works anywhere, full stop.

### Course Awareness Across Lessons

When a lesson is published, the system automatically creates a summary of the entire course structure — what vocabulary has been taught, what questions exist, what files are available. The AI uses this to give answers that reference other lessons and avoid repeating what students already know.

An AI that understands the full course gives far better answers than one that only sees the current page.

### Safety Filters

All student submissions pass through content safety checks that detect harassment, hate speech, self-harm content, and other harmful material. Flagged content is quarantined for instructor review. The system automatically moderates peer review chats and provides instructors with clear policy guidance.

Online learning environments — especially those involving minors — must be safe spaces. Automated detection catches problems before they reach anyone.

## Motivation & Game Mechanics

### Points, Levels & Streaks

Students earn points for completing assignments, practicing, reviewing peers, and discovering hidden surprises. Points add up to levels with named ranks. Daily study streaks encourage consistency, with a "shield" mechanic that forgives one missed day — and warnings when a streak is at risk.

Visible progress and streak mechanics are among the most reliable drivers of daily engagement and long-term retention.

### Achievement Badges

Over 15 badge types plus instructor-created custom badges (with AI-assisted visual design). There are also "anti-badges" — playful debuffs with redemption paths that add humor and second chances. Badges reveal with a coin-flip animation, display on profiles, and instructors can design their own using a visual editor.

Meaningful achievements give students goals beyond grades and celebrate the many different ways effort shows up.

### Story Campaigns & Boss Battles

Instructors can wrap assignments into narrative storylines with chapters that unlock sequentially. "Boss battles" are collaborative challenges where groups work together against a shared goal with high stakes and bonus point multipliers.

Framing schoolwork as a shared adventure turns obligation into excitement and gives students a reason to rally together.

### Skill Trees

Lessons can be organized into prerequisite chains — learn A before you can access B. A visual tree shows students their progress through connected skill nodes, and instructors design the progression paths using a drag-and-drop editor.

Structured progression stops students from skipping ahead before they're ready and gives them a clear path to follow.

### Teams & Leaderboards

Students form small teams with custom crests and team chat. Leaderboards show both individual and team standings with completion grids and fastest-completion highlights. Competition is friendly — designed to motivate, not discourage.

Social accountability and friendly competition push students to try harder — and working in teams teaches skills no quiz can.

### Hidden Surprises

Instructors can hide discoverable secrets throughout the platform — triggered by typing keywords in chat, visiting at certain times, finding hidden links, or earning specific badges. Discoveries reward bonus points and show a celebratory notification.

Surprise and delight keep the experience from going stale, and reward the students who like to explore.

### Locked Content

Instructors can gate lessons behind requirements — earn enough points, collect specific badges, or complete prerequisite modules first. Locked content shows a clear indicator of what's needed and a progress bar toward unlocking it, with an animation when the gate opens.

Controlled access builds anticipation, prevents overwhelm, and gives students something concrete to work toward.

### Celebration Moments

When students level up or complete story chapters, automatic full-screen celebrations appear with confetti bursts and congratulatory messages. Even small victories get "Nailed It" celebrations. All animations respect accessibility settings — users who prefer reduced motion see instant, subtle alternatives.

Celebrating achievements in the moment reinforces the behavior that earned them and makes progress feel genuinely rewarding.

### Avatar Customization

Students unlock new avatar styles as they level up — from basic portraits to detailed characters with glowing effects and even heraldic shields. Higher levels unlock more elaborate customization options, giving students visible status and creative expression.

Self-expression through a personalized avatar builds real identity investment in the platform, and the progression rewards give students something to work toward.

### Visual Themes

Students unlock cosmetic themes (dark mode variants, color schemes) as they progress. They can select preset palettes or mix their own custom colors. Themes apply across the entire interface, making the app feel uniquely theirs.

Personalization creates a sense of ownership, and earning cosmetic rewards adds motivation without touching fairness.

## Behind the Scenes (Infrastructure)

### Automatic File Processing

When instructors upload files, the system automatically processes them in the background — creating thumbnails for images, extracting text from PDFs, generating subtitles for videos, and preparing everything for fast delivery. No manual steps required.

Instructors can upload any file type and trust it will be optimized and ready for students — no follow-up steps needed.

### Fast Global Delivery

All media (images, audio, video) is served through a worldwide content network that loads quickly regardless of where students are located. Video streams securely in adaptive quality, and access is controlled so only authorized students see the content.

Slow-loading media kills engagement. Students everywhere deserve the same fast experience.

### Smart Search

Students and instructors can search across all lessons, files, vocabulary, and questions using natural language. The system understands meaning, not just keywords — so searching "animals that live in water" finds content about fish even if "water" isn't explicitly mentioned.

Finding the right content quickly is what makes a large library of materials actually usable rather than overwhelming.

### Usage Analytics

The platform tracks page performance and learning engagement metrics, aggregates them into dashboards viewable by instructors and admins, and identifies issues like slow pages or low completion rates. Data is enriched with geographic information for multi-region deployments.

Instructors need data to understand what's working — and admins need it to keep the platform running well.

### Timely Reminders

Automatic notifications for approaching due dates, streak-at-risk warnings, peer review invitations, and instructor announcements. Notifications appear in-app with badge counts and a full inbox page. Scheduled checks run periodically to trigger time-sensitive alerts.

Without proactive reminders, students miss deadlines and lose streaks. Instructors also need a reliable way to reach everyone at once.

### Fast Page Loads

Pages load quickly because the system pre-renders content on the server, caches expensive computations, and the browser framework automatically optimizes re-renders. Frequently accessed pages (leaderboards, skill trees) are pre-built and served instantly from cache.

Every second of load time chips away at engagement. Fast pages feel professional and keep students in the flow.

### Multi-Language Support

The entire interface is available in 6 languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese) with all text properly translated, including dates, numbers, and cultural formatting. Switching languages changes everything instantly.

Learners are simply more comfortable — and more effective — when the interface speaks their language.

## Tools for Instructors & Admins

### Sharing Content Between Instructors

Instructors own their lessons by default but can grant access to collaborators for co-editing. A community library lets instructors share templates that others can copy into their own courses. Admins see everything.

Instructors shouldn't have to reinvent every lesson from scratch. Sharing and adapting quality content saves enormous amounts of time.

### Safe Deletion with Recovery

Deleting content doesn't destroy it — items go to a recycle bin where they can be restored. Even "permanently deleted" items are archived (never truly destroyed) so admins can recover critical content if needed.

Accidental deletion of a semester's worth of content would be catastrophic. A recycle bin and archive layer take that anxiety off the table.

### Security Protections

Multiple layers protect against abuse: video streaming requires authentication, the AI can't be tricked into pretending to be an admin, uploaded images are sanitized, real-time connections verify identity, and automated rate limiting prevents spam.

A platform used by students — often minors — has to be hardened against common attack vectors. These layers ensure it is.

### Grade Review Interface

Instructors review student submissions in a read-only view with margin annotations showing which answers were wrong, inline grade editing, keyboard navigation between students, and a timeline showing how the student's answers changed over time.

Efficient grading tools save instructors hours per week, and the answer history helps surface patterns that a single snapshot never would.

### Guided Walkthroughs

New users (both instructors and students) get step-by-step guided tours that highlight features with spotlight effects and explanations. Tours can also be triggered by the AI chatbot when it detects someone is struggling with a feature.

A complex platform needs onboarding. Guided tours cut support requests and get both instructors and students productive faster.

### Background Job Monitor

Admins and instructors can see the status of all background tasks (file processing, AI analysis, video encoding) and retry failed ones or cancel stuck ones. A dashboard shows what's running, what succeeded, and what needs attention.

When an upload fails or processing gets stuck, instructors shouldn't have to guess what happened — they need visibility and control.

### Storage Overview

A visual dashboard showing how much space is used by cached files, downloaded AI models, and saved content. Users can clear specific caches, download content for offline use, or remove AI models they no longer need.

Mobile devices have limited storage, and users deserve to know exactly what the app is keeping — and how to reclaim space when they need it.

### Developer Tools

A hidden panel for developers with a component tree view, state inspector, log viewer, performance profiler, and the ability to send diagnostic reports. Not visible to regular users.

Developers need efficient debugging tools — without them, small issues become expensive investigations.

### Class Management

Instructors create classes, generate join codes for students, assign lessons with due dates, and configure per-class settings. Students join with a code and see their class-grouped assignments. Each class can have different AI behavior, game mechanics, and content.

A simple way to organize students into groups — and control what each group sees and when — is fundamental to running a class well.

### Joining Collaborative Sessions

A single dialog shows all pending invitations — practice sessions, shared workbooks, and peer reviews. Students accept or decline from one place instead of hunting through separate interfaces.

A unified entry point reduces confusion and makes sure students don't accidentally miss collaboration opportunities.

### Consistent Page Layout

Every page shares the same navigation structure — a sidebar on desktop that collapses to a menu on mobile, consistent header, and accessibility features like skip-to-content links. The current page is always highlighted in navigation.

Consistent layout reduces cognitive load and makes the platform feel cohesive — whether you're on a phone, tablet, or desktop.

### Personal Preferences

A settings page where users manage their password, display name, avatar, motion preferences (disable animations for accessibility), color theme, notification preferences, and language — all in one place.

Every user has different needs, and centralizing all these settings in one place makes the platform comfortable for everyone.
