## Create Your First Lesson

The **Editor** is where you create interactive lessons with text, images, videos, quizzes, and more.

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Navigate to:** `📚 Creating Lessons → Editor → Empty Editor`
2. **Try typing:** Click in the editor and type "Welcome to my lesson!"
3. **Format text:** Select text and use the toolbar to make it **bold** or *italic*
4. **Add a heading:** Type `/heading` and press Enter - now your text is a title
5. **Insert a quiz:**
   - Type `/quiz` and press Enter
   - Fill in your question: "What is the capital of Japan?"
   - Add answer choices
   - Mark the correct answer

**🎉 You just created your first lesson!**

**Next steps:**
- Add images with `/image`
- Embed videos with `/youtube`
- Create vocabulary exercises with `/word`
- [Try the full Editor](?path=/docs/📚-creating-lessons-editor--docs)
- [Explore all editor plugins](?path=/docs/🔌-editor-plugins-ai-content-completion--docs)

## Record Dialogue Audio

The **Recording Studio** helps you create conversations with multiple speakers using text-to-speech or your voice.

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Navigate to:** `🎙️ Recording Audio → Recording Studio → Guided Tutorial`
2. **Follow the tutorial** - it walks you through:
   - Adding speakers (characters)
   - Writing their dialogue
   - Generating computer voices
   - Recording your own voice
   - Comparing different takes

**Or try a preset example:**
- [Coffee Shop Dialogue](?path=/story/🎙️-recording-audio-recording-studio--coffee-shop-dialogue) - See a complete conversation
- [Start From Scratch](?path=/story/🎙️-recording-audio-recording-studio--start-from-scratch) - Build your own from zero
- [Guided Tutorial](?path=/story/🎙️-recording-audio-recording-studio--guided-tutorial) - Step-by-step walkthrough

**💡 Tip:** Use text-to-speech first to hear the timing, then record your own voice to replace it!

## Create Vocabulary

Build vocabulary lists with audio pronunciation, images, and example sentences.

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Navigate to:** `📁 Managing Content → Dictionary Editor` *(coming soon to this reorganization)*
2. **Click "Add Word"**
3. **Fill in:**
   - Japanese word: こんにちは
   - Pronunciation (romaji): konnichiwa
   - Definition: Hello, Good afternoon
   - Tags: greeting, basic
4. **Add audio:**
   - Click microphone icon
   - Generate TTS or record yourself
5. **Save** - your word is added!

**Batch import:** You can also import CSV files with multiple words at once.

## Create a Quiz

Design practice questions with multiple formats: multiple choice, short answer, translation, and more.

### Step-by-Step:

**Option 1: Inside a Lesson**
1. Go to `📚 Creating Lessons → Editor → Empty Editor`
2. Type `/quiz` and press Enter
3. Add your question and answers
4. Choose question type (multiple choice, short answer, etc.)

**Option 2: Standalone Question**
1. Go to `📁 Managing Content → Question Editor` *(coming soon)*
2. Click "Add Question"
3. Fill in prompt and answer
4. Add hints or explanations
5. Optionally add audio or images

## Use AI Assistant

Get help creating content, generating ideas, and translating with the AI chat assistant.

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Navigate to:** `💬 AI Assistant → Chat → Getting Started`
2. **Type a question** like:
   - "Help me create a lesson about Japanese seasons"
   - "Generate 5 quiz questions about hiragana"
   - "Give me ideas for teaching Japanese verb conjugation"
3. **Press Enter** - the AI responds with helpful content
4. **Ask follow-ups** to refine the ideas
5. **Copy and paste** AI suggestions into your lessons

**Example Prompts:**
- "Create a dialogue between two students meeting for the first time"
- "List 20 common Japanese restaurant words"
- "Explain the difference between は and が particles"

## Translate Content

Use the AI to translate between English and Japanese.

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Navigate to:** `💬 AI Assistant → Chat → Translation Helper`
2. **Type your translation request:**
   - "Translate 'How are you?' to Japanese with romaji"
   - "What's the polite way to say 'thank you' in Japanese?"
   - "How do you write 'spring' in kanji?"
3. **Get instant translations** with:
   - Kanji/Hiragana/Katakana
   - Romaji pronunciation
   - Formal vs. casual versions
   - Cultural context

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Can I undo mistakes?**  
A: Yes! Press `Ctrl/Cmd + Z` to undo, `Ctrl/Cmd + Y` to redo.

**Q: Where is my content saved?**  
A: In Storybook, content is temporary. In the real app, everything saves to your AWS account automatically.

**Q: How do I add my own images?**  
A: Go to `📁 Managing Content → File Manager` and upload your files.

**Q: Can I export my lessons?**  
A: Yes! Most stories have a "Download" or "Export JSON" option.

**Q: Does this work offline?**  
A: Most features work offline. Only the AI assistant requires internet.
